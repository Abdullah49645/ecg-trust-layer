What kind of image does this accept?
  • One lead, drawn as a dark trace on light paper with a standard pink/red grid (1 mm and 5 mm lines).
  • Standard speed and gain are assumed: 25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV.
  • Roughly frontal views. If the grid or trace cannot be read, the result is “unable”. The tool does not guess.

Reproduce the evaluation

This runs the real pipeline on your machine: render → degrade → digitize → score → statistics. Nothing is replayed. Progress below is reported by the running job.

Quick preset is a reproducibility check, not a replacement for the full test: it uses the frozen trust model on 10 held-out PTB-XL test-fold records, 8 image conditions (about 30–60 s). The full from-scratch run (all 21 conditions, 170 records) is python scripts/run_evaluation.py --preset full and takes tens of minutes. “Re-derive full statistics” re-fits the model and re-evaluates from the bundled per-image results (about 4–5 min); it reports stage-level progress only, and re-checks the shipped full results.
